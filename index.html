Why we exist

We just wanted a good momo place nearby

MomoSomo started because the momos we liked were always a 40-minute drive away. So we picked one thing — momos, done a few different ways — and decided to be genuinely good at it instead of average at everything.

The dough is made fresh, the fillings are prepped daily, and the chutney is never a shortcut bottle. It's a short menu on purpose.

The counter

What it's actually like to walk in

Small, warm, and usually smells like steam and garlic by noon.

  • Momos steamed to order, not sitting under a lamp
  • Chutney made twice a day in small batches
  • Seating for groups of two to eight
  • Cutting chai on tap, always hot
  • Takeaway packed properly — nothing soggy by the time you're home
  • Evenings get a slower, quieter crowd
